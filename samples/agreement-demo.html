Client Agreement Template — Portfolio Demonstration

Creating clear, reusable client documents

This demonstration illustrates how I can prepare, format and organise professional business agreement templates.

Demonstration only: This is a fictional example for portfolio purposes. It is not a signed agreement or a legally reviewed contract. Businesses should obtain appropriate legal advice before using agreement terms.

SERVICE AGREEMENT

Example document | Version 1.0

Service Provider: Example Virtual Assistant
Client: Example Small Business (fictional)
Effective Date: To be completed

1. Scope of Services

The service provider may assist with agreed administrative activities, such as:

  • Data entry and document organisation
  • Email and inbox administration
  • Formatting business documents
  • Preparing task lists and progress updates

2. Responsibilities

The service provider will carry out agreed tasks using the information and instructions supplied by the client. The client will provide necessary materials, approvals and feedback.

3. Payment Terms

The agreed project fee, payment method, invoice schedule and due date will be recorded here before the agreement is finalised.

4. Confidentiality

Both parties should define how confidential business information will be accessed, handled, stored and protected.

5. Changes and Approvals

Any changes to the scope, deliverables or deadlines should be documented and approved by both parties.

6. Signatures

Signature fields are provided as a formatting demonstration only.

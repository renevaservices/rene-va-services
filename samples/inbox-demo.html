My example process

  1. Review incoming messages and identify time-sensitive requests.
  2. Group emails by urgency, topic and required action.
  3. Prepare clear draft replies for approval.
  4. Record follow-up tasks and important dates.
  5. Maintain an organised inbox using consistent folders or labels.

Outcome illustrated: A clearer inbox, visible priorities and an easier daily workflow.

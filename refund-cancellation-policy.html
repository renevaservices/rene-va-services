Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Monthly plans

You may cancel a monthly plan by giving written notice before your next billing date. The plan stays active until the end of the month already paid for, and no further invoices are issued.

Fees for a month that has already started are not refundable, as time is reserved for your work in advance.

Per-project work

If you cancel before work has started, any amount paid is refunded in full. If you cancel after work has started, you pay for the work completed up to that point and any balance is refunded.

Completed and delivered projects are not refundable. If a deliverable does not match the agreed brief, let us know and it will be revised at no extra cost.

Digital products

Because digital products are delivered instantly, they are non-refundable once downloaded, unless the file is faulty, in which case a working copy is provided.
